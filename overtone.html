Harmonics

Resonances

A resonance is a frequency that keeps showing up as a peak for most of this time, like a room mode, a fan, mains hum or a ringing string. Shorter memory reacts faster.

Excite the room

Play a sound out of the speaker while the microphone listens, so the room's own resonances light up. Turn the volume down first.

Input

Drag up and down to move through frequencies, pinch or scroll to zoom, double-tap to reset. Keys: Space freezes, H hides the controls, Esc closes the probe.
